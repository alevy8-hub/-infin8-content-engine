1. Five Habits of Calm Creators

1. Batch your work

Creators who batch their filming save setup time. One long session beats five short ones.

2. Reuse every idea

A single long post can become 3.5 hours of short-form material. Version 2.0 of an idea is often better than the first.

3. Protect your mornings

Most creative work happens before noon. Guard that window.
